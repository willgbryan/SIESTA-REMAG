\section*{Data and scripts}

Everything needed to check these numbers is in the SIESTA-REMAG source tree, in the
folder \nolinkurl{benchmarks/gpu-performance-hcp-co}.
\nolinkurl{data/records.jsonl} gives each run's wall time, iteration count and final
results. \nolinkurl{scripts/bench.py} generated the inputs and ran every calculation, and
\nolinkurl{scripts/analyze.py} drew every figure and computed every number quoted here.
\nolinkurl{data/machine.txt} has the SHA-256 hashes of the three SIESTA executables and of
REMAG's GPU library. The REMAG build is commit \texttt{2b8a2c0} of SIESTA-REMAG plus
uncommitted changes, identified by those hashes. Full inputs, outputs and SIESTA timer
trees for every run are kept in a separate archive of about \SI{1}{GB}.
